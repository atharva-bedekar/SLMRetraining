How the Project Evolved

Six controlled iterations moved the model from unusable raw text to reliable, source-grounded clinical extraction.

1. Feasibility Pilot

1,325 notes · 10 val

Confirmed the 270M model can learn structured medical output reliably when trained in full numerical precision (FP32) on a single T4 GPU.

2. First Full-Schema Run

1,325 notes · 30 dev

Trained on the full medical schema. Exposed noisy training labels and leftover prompt wording that needed cleanup.

3. Focused 30-Field Schema (V3)

1,325 notes · 100 frozen dev

Simplified the output contract to 30 strict fields on a locked 100-note evaluation set. Reached 95/100 valid format and strong gains across 400 AI judge scores.

4. Scaled Full Schema (5,000 Notes)

5,000 notes · 100 frozen dev

Tested 3.8× more training data on the exact same 100 notes. Reached 96/100 valid format and lifted completeness to 55.0%.

5. Clean Regenerated Labels (V4 · 1,325)

1,325 notes · 100 frozen dev

Rebuilt the 1,325 training labels directly from raw notes using Gemini 3.8 Flash and automated repair rules. Lifted accuracy gain to +21.4% (53.7%) and completeness to 56.1%.

6. Scaled Clean Labels (V4 · 5,000 · Latest)

5,000 notes · 100 frozen dev

Scaled the exact V4 clean-label pipeline to 5,000 notes (1,325 V4 + 3,675 regenerated V2 notes). Achieved 97/100 strict schema validity, 40.17% Strict F1 / 55.27% Soft F1 (1,636 exact matches), and 61.9% fidelity (+29.6%) / 62.5% coverage (+26.8%).

How the AI Judge scores outputs & 20-case decoding tune-up

Each of the 100 evaluation notes is scored by an independent AI judge (GPT-6 Luna or Gemini 4 Hydrogen) directly against the original clinical note, rather than trusting synthetic reference labels blindly:

  • Accuracy (Source Fidelity): Checks whether every claim in the model's output is supported by the clinical note (drug, dose, negation, timing, and patient vs. relative).
  • Completeness (Source Coverage): Checks how many clinically material facts from the note were captured accurately.
  • 20-Case Decoding Tune-Up: Switching from fixed rp=1.08 to clean generation (rp=1.00 with rp=1.08 fallback on loops) lifted 20-case schema validity from 16/20 → 19/20 (V4) and 15/20 → 18/20 (V3), and was confirmed on all 100 notes in V4 (rp=1.00_fb1.08).
  • Statistical Confidence: 95% confidence intervals were computed via 100,000 paired bootstrap resamples; every lower bound remains well above zero.

How It Works & What's Next

Training runs on cloud GPUs; all formatting checks, AI judging, and spot-checks run locally on verified exports.

Current Boundaries

  • Proven: Fine-tuning lifts Gemma 3 270M from 0% to 92–97% valid structured output, reaching 61.9% fidelity and 62.5% coverage on V4-5000.
  • Not yet proven: Real-world clinical accuracy—no clinician review has been conducted yet, and the official test split remains untouched.

Next Controlled Steps

  • Clinician Review: Calibrate automated AI judge scores (61.9% fidelity / 62.5% coverage) against blinded clinician grading on a stratified sample.
  • Final Held-Out Test Run: Evaluate the locked V4-5000 checkpoint (rp=1.00_fb1.08) once on the untouched official test split after clinician calibration.
